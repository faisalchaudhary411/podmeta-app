How to Add Cover Art to Podcast MP3 Files

Learn Apple Podcast and Spotify image standards for embedding artwork directly into ID3 tags.

Recommended Cover Art Specifications

  • Dimensions: 1400x1400 to 3000x3000 pixels (1:1 square).
  • Format: JPEG or PNG format.
  • Color Space: RGB color profile.
  • Embedding: Tagged under the APIC ID3 frame.
