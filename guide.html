How to Tag & Verify Podcast MP3s

Step 1: Uploading Audio & Cover Art

Drag your MP3 file into the upload dropzone on the homepage. The tool will parse existing ID3 tags automatically. Next, upload your preferred JPG or PNG image to serve as embedded artwork.

Step 2: Entering Metadata & Auto-Renaming

Fill out the Episode Title (`TIT2`), Host Name (`TPE1`), Show Title (`TALB`), and Year (`TYER`). The file will automatically download renamed according to your Episode Title to instantly signal successful tag embedding.

Step 3: Verifying Tags on Your Device

  • Windows PC: Right-click the MP3 → Properties → Details tab.
  • Mac: Right-click the MP3 → Get Info → More Info section.
  • Android / iOS: Open the file in Apple Music, VLC, or Files app to inspect artwork and titles.
