Frequently Asked Questions

Why are ID3 tags important for podcasts?

When users download audio files for offline playback, ID3 tags ensure your artwork, title, and brand credits stay attached to the file regardless of the media player used.

Can I edit multiple MP3 files at once?

Yes. Select multiple files in the dropzone to apply uniform host, show, and year metadata across all tracks, exported cleanly in a single ZIP file.
